Hestia household budget theory

Nothing here leaves your browser. Taxes are a rough estimate (standard deduction, 401(k) and health premiums are optional inputs; no deductions or credits) — good for comparing offers, not for filing. Percentages are of take-home pay.

Import what you actually spent

Pick a categorized CSV with an Amount and a Category column (a Date column is optional). The file is read right here in your browser. Individual transactions are never stored or sent anywhere; only per-category totals are kept.

Each CSV category's amounts are netted (a refund cancels a purchase). It counts as spending only when the net is positive; tick flip if that category shows spending as negative amounts. Choose where each one goes, or ignore transfers.
